Quantity

How quantity moves price

Unit price drops fast at first, then settles. Tooling, sampling, and setup amortize across the run — the bigger the run, the less each piece carries.

1–5 pcs 100% Retail / baseline
10–25 ~62% First volume break
50–100 ~57% Tooling pays off
100+ ~50% Half of retail
500+ ~40% Production rhythm
1000+ ~30% Practical floor

Indicative ranges only. Real per-unit pricing depends on geometry, finish, and the suppliers we shortlist for your piece.

Material

How material moves price

Premium is our default tier — the 1× baseline. Budget and Mid-market unlock further savings; Bespoke adds a premium for hand-selected stock.

Budget 0.35× MDF, particleboard, laminate, low-density foam. Further savings.
Mid-market 0.55× Plywood, veneer, rubberwood, 1.8 lb foam, acceptable joinery.
Premium 1× Solid wood, kiln-dried hardwood, performance fabric, reinforced joinery. Default.
Bespoke 2× Hand-selected stock, hand-applied finishes, custom hardware.

Multipliers are vs. our Premium baseline. Real per-unit cost depends on geometry, finish, and sourcing.

Timeline

How timeline moves price

Tight is our default pace — the 1× baseline. Normal and Flexible let us batch into runs already on the line; Rush and Emergency buy queue priority and air freight.

Emergency 2.4× All-hands, every shortcut on the table.
Rush 1.6× Queue priority and air freight.
Tight 1× Default. Faster than normal, no heroics.
Normal 0.80× Our honest range — further savings.
Flexible 0.55× Batched into runs already on the line.

Multipliers are vs. our Tight baseline. Tight ≈ 3–4 wks personal, 6–10 wks production.
