The lockup

Ariadne

Mark + wordmark, vertically centred, gap: 12px. The mark box is 30px in product use. Wordmark is EB Garamond italic 500.

Mark only

Drawn at a 24×24 viewBox: three concentric arcs (r = 9, 6, 3) each with an offset gap, plus a 1.1 r centre dot. Stroke is currentColor at 1.35, round caps and joins, so it scales cleanly and recolours with text.

Clear space

Keep at least one mark-height of empty space on every side (the dashed box). In the product it sits at top: clamp(24px,3.4vh,42px) / left: clamp(28px,3.8vw,50px).

Don't

Don't recolour the markIt's paper-white #fff5e1 with a spark/accent glow. The glow does the colour work.
Don't place on lightThis is a luminous mark for dark canvases only — it disappears on white.
Don't set the wordmark uprightThe wordmark is always italic. Roman Garamond is not the brand.
Don't rebuild the spacingUse the lockup as one unit; never re-kern mark-to-word.
