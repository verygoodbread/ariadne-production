Motion

One easing curve, used everywhere. Differences come from duration, not from swapping curves.

The curve

--ease: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) — a fast, confident start that settles softly. It is the only easing in the system; every transition and keyframe references it.
