Typography

An editorial serif voice carried by one workhorse sans for product UI.

Typefaces

Serif · display & body EB Garamond — and its italic

--serif: "EB Garamond", "Cormorant Garamond", Georgia, serif — every headline, the wordmark, and all body copy. Italic is a primary voice here (prompts, leads, the CTA), not just emphasis.

Sans · product UI Inter — labels, eyebrows, data

--sans: "Inter", system-ui, sans-serif — scoped to the chrome inside the visualisers (stats, captions, keycaps) so product UI reads as UI, not prose.
